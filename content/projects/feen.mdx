**feen** is a solution for the clothing field that is accesible on both android and ios platforms. it allows tailors, retailers and end-users to buy, sell and exchange their second-hand clothing products on the platform. it's built with donation, sustainability and fashion in mind.

### my role

- digital marketing

## services included

- **Buy, Sell and Exchange Second-Hand Clothing**  
   A marketplace where users can give pre-loved clothing a new life.

- **Tailor and Retailer Participation**  
   Tailors and retailers can showcase, sell, and exchange clothing products with a wider audience.

- **Mobile Access on iOS and Android**  
   A convenient experience designed for users on both major mobile platforms.

- **Sustainable Fashion**  
   Encourages circular fashion by reducing textile waste and extending the life of clothing.

- **Donation-Focused Features**  
   Supports clothing donation and community-based reuse, helping make fashion more accessible.
