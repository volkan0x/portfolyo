feedbird is a social media management platform that provides end-to-end design and digital marketing services for small and medium sized businesses.

## services included

- **Social Media Strategy**  
   Clear, goal-focused plans built around your brand, audience, and business objectives.

- **Creative Design & Content Production**  
   Social media graphics, branded visuals, motion content, and campaign assets that stand out.

- **Content Planning & Management**  
   Content calendars, post scheduling, and consistent publishing across your social channels.

-  **Digital Advertising**  
   Targeted paid campaigns designed to improve reach, engagement, leads, and sales.

-  **Performance Tracking & Optimisation**  
   Regular reporting and ongoing improvements based on campaign and audience performance.
