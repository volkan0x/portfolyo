## services included:

- Room reservations — Users can select check-in and check-out dates, choose guests, and start a booking.
- Accommodation options — The site showcases different room types, such as deluxe rooms, suites, and bungalows.
- Restaurant and bar services — Navigation links indicate dining and bar options.
- Spa and wellness — Promotes relaxation and wellness experiences.
- Activities and experiences — Includes food, hiking, water sports, and other leisure activities.
- Event and couple packages — Offers services designed for couples, celebrations, or special occasions.
- Destination information — Helps visitors explore the surrounding area and nearby attractions.
- Guest reviews — Displays TripAdvisor feedback to build trust.
- Promotions and offers — Highlights special deals and packages.
- Contact and inquiry form — Allows visitors to send questions or reservation requests.

**flora garden is a five star hotel located at the Antalya/Turkiye.**
